Public Service Announcement · Out-of-Home

"Going It Alone Is Over": YourPeer Winter 2026 PSA

A 12-week public service announcement campaign running on screens across New York City via the F.Y. Eye PSA Network. Directed by Gavilán Longino; visual design by Di Lu. The campaign reminded New Yorkers that no one should have to face challenges without support, and encouraged use and sharing of YourPeer, a free, peer-validated resource connecting people to trustworthy help across shelter, food, clothing, legal services, and more.

Campaign Display — Screen Recording View in Gallery

The full PSA ad creative and campaign copy are available in the F.Y. Eye PSA Gallery.

About the PSA

"Going it alone is over": the campaign's headline acknowledged the difficulty of finding reliable information about services and positioned YourPeer as a trusted, peer-validated alternative. Streetlives, the nonprofit behind YourPeer, builds digital tools with people who have lived experience to make finding help easier, more accurate, and more human.

Political Advertising · Behavioral Economics

Turning Political Rage into Strategic Engagement

The Challenge: Transform voter anger across party lines into constructive political action through a zero-budget Facebook ad campaign targeting heavily Democratic markets.

The Strategy: Applied behavioral economics, including loss aversion, emotional priming, and cognitive dissonance, in a 40-second video that acknowledged widespread political frustration before redirecting it toward collaborative solutions. Used anger as an entry point, visual imagery of political conflict to enhance viewer identification, then reversed both audio and visual elements to guide viewers from rage to resolve.

Hostile Stakeholder Conversion: Using Socratic questioning, converted several hostile interactions into constructive dialogue by forcing participants to confront their own core arguments. This campaign contributed to the broader 25% hostile-to-supportive conversion rate across the full 1Party.org initiative.
